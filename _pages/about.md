Hi, welcome! I am a Research Fellow at Singapore Management University (SMU). Before joining SMU, I was a Senior Research Engineer at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), where I worked with Prof. [Yang Liu](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/yangliu/) and Prof. [LAM, Kwok Yan](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/kwokyan.lam/) on applied research at the intersection of cybersecurity and AI. 

My research centers on encrypted network traffic analysis and security-oriented machine learning, including encrypted traffic representation learning and classification, anomaly detection under data drift, multi-flow contextual modeling, and building practical systems for traffic monitoring and APT analysis. Recently, I’ve also been exploring LLM-assisted APT detection and explanation. I collaborate closely with Prof. [Yue Duan](https://yueduan.github.io/lab.html) , and I enjoy turning real-world security challenges into measurable benchmarks and deployable solutions
